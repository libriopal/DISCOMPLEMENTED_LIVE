# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 21, 2026

## Data We Collect

### Account Data

- Email address (for authentication and notifications)
- Hashed password (never stored in plaintext)
- Account creation date

### Interaction Data (Ephemeral)

- User prompts and AI responses (stored for 30 days, then automatically deleted)
- Pipeline execution logs (stored for 30 days, then automatically deleted)
- This data is used to calibrate the AI system and optimize value delivery

### Usage Data

- Credits consumed per session
- Applications generated
- Session timestamps

## How We Use Your Data

- **Service delivery:** Process your prompts and generate applications
- **System optimization:** Calibrate AI models to improve value delivery rate
- **Abuse prevention:** Detect and prevent prohibited uses (see Acceptable Use Policy)
- **Billing:** Track credit usage and process payments

## What We Do NOT Do

- We do NOT sell your data to third parties
- We do NOT use your data for advertising
- We do NOT permanently store interaction content (30-day TTL)
- We do NOT train external models on your data
- We do NOT share your data without legal compulsion

## Data Retention

| Data Type           | Retention Period             |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Account credentials | Until account deletion       |
| Interaction logs    | 30 days (automatic deletion) |
| Generated code      | Until user deletes it        |
| Payment records     | 7 years (tax compliance)     |
| Abuse flags         | Until account resolution     |

## Your Rights

### GDPR (EU/EEA)

- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

### CCPA (California)

- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale (we do not sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination

### Exercising Your Rights

Email: privacy@discomplemented.com

## Data Security

- All secrets stored via Cloudflare Worker secrets (encrypted at rest)
- HTTPS/TLS for all connections
- No plaintext password storage (bcrypt hashing)
- Row-level security on all database queries
- Regular security audits via GitHub CodeQL

## Children's Privacy

Discomplement is not intended for users under 13 (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn we have, we will delete it immediately.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy with reasonable notice. Material changes will be announced via email or in-app notification.
