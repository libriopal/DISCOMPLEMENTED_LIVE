04 — Architect Recommendations
Reorganizations for Scale
Collapse 15 → 3 nav zones
Grouping by intent (Workspace / Intelligence / System) keeps the top nav to ~5 items and matches the lifecycle mental model.
Lattice as persistent rail
The Memory Lattice appears as a collapsible right rail on every Intelligence screen — always showing alignment context, not just its own page.
Approval as a modal layer
Human-in-loop gates are a cross-cutting overlay reachable from any stage — reducing screen count while surfacing pending approvals everywhere.
