Cross-Screen — Responsive Principles
One System, Every Screen
Rails collapse, content persists
4-col side rails become bottom sheets or accordions on mobile — never hidden. The 8-col primary content always stays first in the reading order.
Sidebar → bottom tabs
The desktop icon-rail nav converts to a 5-item bottom tab bar under 768px, keeping primary destinations one thumb-tap away.
Lattice stays first-class
On small screens the lattice becomes a tappable summary card that opens full-screen — spatial context is never dropped, only deferred.
Every state has a design
Empty, loading (skeleton), error, and permission states are specified per screen — no blank frames. Focus ring #4c6ee6 2px is consistent everywhere.
